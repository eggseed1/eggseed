the case for little records

october 22, 2026 / filed under: little notes

* -- * -- * -- * -- *

there is something special about the seven-inch record. two songs, a paper sleeve, a crackle at the beginning. and then you flip the thing over and start again.

country 45s make a beautiful way of discovering music. a hit on one side, sometimes an even better song tucked away on the other.

this site will become a running inventory of that little world.
