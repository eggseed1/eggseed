original collection notes

I’m a big fan of rockabilly that flirts more with the “hillbilly” side than the “rock” side … and despite its title, the A-side track “Gotta Rock” definitely hits that sweet spot. This particular copy is a 1970s reissue from Olympic Records, originally released in 1956 by Stardale Records, a label started by Dale Davis himself out in Oklahoma. Prob has some personal significance to him as this was the very first single he put out on his own label.
Bought at @rooky_ricardos_records thanks!
