original collection notes

I’m sure a lot of you can relate to this fine tune given the current economic situation 😪 Buck Griffin certainly could. Music always had to take a back seat to earning a living. He spent time digging pipeline ditches in Kansas and eventually worked his way up to become a driller in the oil fields.

Luckily, the oil fields were surrounded by fantastic honky tonk bars that gave him a way back into music. During the day, he worked in the fields, and at night, he performed at the local bars. He struggled financially throughout his career and never quite made it big, but certainly he was a talented songwriter and performer.

What I love most is the charm that comes through in all of his songs, carrying this distinctive southern twang.
