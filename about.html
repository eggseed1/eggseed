what's in the shoebox?

mostly country records, particularly 7-inch 45 rpm singles. also country music history, thrifted magazines, interesting scans, photographs, and occasional writing.

a few interests

  • honky-tonk, hillbilly, old-time and western swing
  • record labels, matrix numbers, and sleeve design
  • forgotten singles and roadside ephemera
  • the homemade internet
